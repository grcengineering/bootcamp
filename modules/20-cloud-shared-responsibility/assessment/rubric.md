# Rubric — Whose Control Is It?

Every criterion is **Pass / Not-yet**. A single Not-yet routes the learner to the named §, not the
whole module. Pass-rates per criterion are the maintenance telemetry (Loop 3).

| # | Criterion | Pass means | Not-yet routes to |
|---|-----------|------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Name the two halves | States security OF the cloud (provider) vs security IN the cloud (you) and why the split decides who can evidence a control — `mapping_correct` on the two anchor cards | §3, §4.1 |
| 2 | Map controls to the right party | Places each control on YOU or PROVIDER for the stated service model — `mapping_correct` against the cited matrix | §4.1, §4.2 |
| 3 | Show the boundary migrate | Re-derives the migrating cards at a second service model (the slider moves the line) — `mapping_correct` after a slider change | §4.2, §5.1 |
| 4 | Sort the planes | Places management-API actions on the control plane and workload traffic on the data plane — `mapping_correct` | §4.3, §5.2 |
| 5 | Place the THROUGHOUT control | Puts audit logging in BOTH plane zones (management + data-access events) — `mapping_correct` | §4.3 |
| 6 | Locate at-rest ownership | States who owns "encrypted at rest" under IaaS (you produce) vs SaaS (you inherit via CUEC) — `mapping_correct` on the migrating card + the note | §4.4, §4.5, §5.1 |
| 7 | Catch the misattribution | Identifies the SaaS claim that says "enforce" where the provider owns it and rewrites it to align ownership and evidence | §4.6, §5.3 |
| 8 | Ownership note | Writes a per-control note naming who is responsible, what evidence each party produces, and the NIST family (SC-28; AC-2/AC-3) | §2, §4.7, §5.4 |
