# Risk Engineer (first-party + third-party)

**The object it engineers:** the **risk models and the risk/vendor data pipeline** — risk made
quantified, current, and decision-grade.

## Jobs-to-be-Done
- Replace qualitative heatmaps with quantified, continuously-updated, $-denominated risk.
- Make third-party / vendor risk a continuous monitoring *pipeline*, not an annual questionnaire.
- Produce decision-support for remediation arbitration (not audit evidence).

## Role Spine (deep)
**Quantitative risk** (FAIR, Monte Carlo, *How to Measure Anything in Cyber Risk*) · risk-register-
as-code · control-effectiveness telemetry · **third-party/vendor risk** (vendor inventory
automation, continuous vendor monitoring, automated questionnaire intake/scoring, SBOM/supply-chain,
concentration risk). Deep on: 05, 06, 07, 11, 14; working on the Common Core, 13.

## Role Capstone
Build a continuously-updated **quantified risk model for one first-party risk** *and* one
**automated continuous-monitoring pipeline for a third-party vendor**, both emitting a
loss-exposure metric.

> Explicitly distinct from Compliance — do **not** make risk a compliance control. Risk produces
> decision-support; Compliance produces audit evidence. (Corpus-validated: Risk was well-represented.)
